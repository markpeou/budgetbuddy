          About Budget Buddy

          A free place to get clear answers to life’s money questions: what things really cost, what’s left, and what changes if life does.

          How the numbers work

          • Everything is converted to a monthly figure first. Weekly amounts are ×52÷12, fortnightly ×26÷12, quarterly ÷3 and yearly ÷12.
          • Left over is your monthly income minus your expenses, minus what you set aside for savings and goals.
          • Savings & goals are percentages of your income. The starting split (50% expenses, 30% long-term savings, 10% emergency fund, 10% investments) is only a suggestion until you change it.
          • The journey calculators use the same figures. Essentials are your fixed costs. Runway rounds down to whole months; time to reach a target rounds up.
          • Each expense can be in its own currency. Totals are converted using daily exchange rates from open.er-api.com, and show “—” if rates can’t be loaded rather than guessing.

          Your data

          • No accounts and no database. What you enter and the scenarios you save live in this browser only.
          • Clearing your browser data removes them. Nothing is sold or shared.
          • If you use receipt reading, the photo is sent to be read and then discarded. Only the amount, place, date and category are kept.

          What it isn’t

          Budget Buddy helps you understand your options. It isn’t financial advice and doesn’t tell you what to do.
