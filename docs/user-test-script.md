# Budget Buddy: 20-minute user test

For one person at a time, on their own phone if possible. Don't help unless they're stuck for more than a minute; note where they hesitate.

## Before you start (1 min)
- "There are no wrong answers. We're testing the site, not you."
- Ask them to think out loud.
- Ask: "Roughly what comes in and goes out for you each month?" (They can use made-up numbers.)

## Tasks

1. **First look (2 min).** Open the homepage. *"What do you think this site does? Where would you start?"*
   - Watch for: do they pick a question, a journey, or the budget planner?

2. **Budget (5 min).** *"Put in your monthly income and three or four regular costs."*
   - Watch for: finding the cost groups, frequency (e.g. a yearly bill), currency.
   - Ask: "How much do you have left over each month? Where did you see that?"

3. **Save it (1 min).** *"You'll come back to this next week. Make sure it's kept."*
   - Watch for: noticing the save prompt; naming the scenario.

4. **A life question (3 min).** *"Could you afford to take three months off?"*
   - Ask: "What's the difference between the two answers?" (today's spending vs only what you need)
   - Ask: "Which costs did we treat as must-pay? Is that right for you?"

5. **A projection (3 min).** Pick one that fits them: trip, debt, home loan or investing.
   - Ask: "In your own words, what is it telling you?" "Do you trust this number? Why / why not?"
   - Watch for: noticing the chart, the 'How we worked it out' figures, and the assumptions.

6. **Compare (3 min).** *"Change your rent as if you'd moved, save it as a second scenario, then compare the two."*
   - Watch for: finding Compare scenarios; reading the Difference column.

## Wrap-up (2 min)
- "What was the most useful thing you saw?"
- "What was confusing or felt like too much?"
- "Would you come back to this? What would make you come back?"
- "On a scale of 1–5, how confident are you in the numbers?"
